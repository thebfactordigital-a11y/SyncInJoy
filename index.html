MY JOURNEY

Why I Teach

I didn't come to yoga ready to teach. I came in 2019, in the middle of a tough chapter of my life, looking for a way out of my own head.

What I found was yoga - not as a quick fix, but as a path that slowly showed me how to come back to myself.

Over the years, I trained deeply across different traditions and three places, before I felt ready to call this my life's work.

Yoga isn't just asana - it asks who you are beneath the layers. I still walk this path every day.
